## A place in Songlines Community Choir, Spring term, starting the first week of January 2027
Lot: 1
Starting bid: 10
Donated by: Bruce & Rebecca Knight
Photos: images/songlines.jpeg

worth (£100)

Songlines is Leamington and Warwick's popular and long-running community choir, led by Bruce Knight, and founded in the year 2000.  Songlines sings a variety of uplifting songs from around the world, mostly in acappella 4-part harmony, (with no backing tracks or piano), and you don't need to be able to read music.  Sessions are generally fun and upbeat, with performances 2 or 3 times a year.  There is a waiting list to join the group, and winning this auction enables you to jump the queue.

You will be able to join either the Tues group (Warwick, starts 5 Jan) or the Weds group (Leamington, starts 6 Jan).
